# Gemini Robotics 2: Whole-Body Intelligence

An embodied-model announcement covering whole-body control, dexterity, and multi-robot coordination; the release does not establish a new RL algorithm.

First public date: 2026-07-30. Reviewed: 2026-09-17.

Classification: Embodied context · VLA and embodied reasoning · not classified as an RL method.

Status: Official model announcement.

## Method

Google DeepMind describes a VLA, an embodied-reasoning model, and an on-device variant, with demonstrations involving humanoids and bi-arm robots.

## Evidence and limits

These are developer-reported capabilities. The inspected announcement does not identify an RL training recipe sufficient to attribute the demonstrated gains to RL. Demonstrations and access announcements are not independent benchmark reproduction. Findings are author-reported; this project has not independently reproduced them.
