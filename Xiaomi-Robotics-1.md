# Xiaomi-Robotics-1: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models with over 100K Hours of Real-World Trajectories

A large-scale VLA pretraining and adaptation study provides context for embodied RL, without establishing that its gains come from reinforcement learning.

First public date: 2026-07-16. Reviewed: 2026-09-17.

Classification: Embodied context · data scaling · foundation policy.

Status: Preprint; revision checked: 2026-07-22.

## Method

The authors describe UMI-collected manipulation trajectories, automated language labeling, and a pretraining/post-training recipe for language-conditioned robot policies.

## Evidence and limits

Evaluation includes physical mobile manipulation and simulation benchmarks. Dataset volume and reported benchmark results are author claims. The described post-training should not automatically be relabeled RL. Findings are author-reported; this project has not independently reproduced them.
