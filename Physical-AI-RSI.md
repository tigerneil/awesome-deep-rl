# Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Evaluation

A survey and evaluation framework separates persistent learner updates from inherited improver revisions across embodied policies, world models, skills, and harnesses.

First public date: 2026-10-28. Reviewed: 2026-11-06.

Classification: Embodied context · survey and evaluation rubric · self-improving physical AI.

Status: Preprint survey.

## Method

The review distinguishes the learner state (policies, world models, reusable skills) from the improver (data acquisition, feedback construction, and update procedures) and introduces an L0–L3 evidence rubric: task-local adaptation (L0), persistent capability gain across resets (L1), inherited process revision (L2), and verified improver gain under matched starts and budgets (L3).

## Evidence and limits

This is a synthesis and evaluation methodology rather than a new control algorithm. It highlights that most current embodied self-improvement demonstrates L0 or L1 gains, while verifying L3 improver gains requires controlled comparisons from shared learner initializations.
