# Deep RL frontier guide — 2026

A curated research update checked through **November 7, 2026**, with emphasis on top AI conference reinforcement learning (NeurIPS 2026, ICML 2026, ICLR 2026, CoRL 2026, RSS 2026, AAAI 2026), embodied RL, and agentic RL. Dates below are first arXiv submissions or explicitly labeled official release dates, not search-engine crawl dates. Selection favors clear methods and traceable sources; it is not an exhaustive ranking.

> **How to use this guide:** start with the dated update, open a research note for method/evaluation/artifact details, then compare with an older baseline. The [interactive homepage](index.html#library) supports search, saved reading lists, and automatic online preprint syncing. All new results are author-reported, not independently reproduced here.

## November 2026 update: embodied and agentic RL

**Synthesis of the sources below:** pretrained robot policies increasingly provide a starting point for targeted RL. Across the 2026 conference cycle (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CoRL, RSS, AAAI) and September–November 2026 releases, work has expanded from frozen-VLA residuals and hallucination-aware world models into stagewise VLM-to-contact residual RL, latent noise bridging for diffusion policies, training-time world-model preference distillation, object-aware humanoid loco-manipulation priors, non-episodic reset-free practice, subtask-gated long-horizon credit assignment, and RL-based low-level inverse kinematics. In parallel, language-agent and core conference RL target reward budgeting against premature convergence, non-stationary rubric curricula, runtime episodic memory RL, massively parallel safe RL, and relative value learning. These are complementary research directions, not evidence of a universally best recipe.

### Evaluation and adjacent embodied developments

- **[BEAKER](BEAKER.md), November 4, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight)** — an expert-curated benchmark of 1,000 QA instances across 11 image/video task types in Self-Driving Chemical Laboratories, showing that 28 evaluated MLLMs still struggle with fine-grained laboratory affordance grounding, feasible trajectories, and safety norms. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911).
- **[CRONOS](CRONOS.md), November 3, 2026** — a ManiSkill3 shared-scene benchmark studying non-episodic multi-task RL fine-tuning of pretrained VLAs under infrequent scene resets. It shows that naive non-episodic fine-tuning degrades under object dispersion and pose drift unless paired with targeted resets. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05080).
- **[MESA](MESA.md), November 3, 2026 (CoRL 2026)** — single-arm (`MESA`) and bimanual (`BiMESA`) evaluation suites isolating spatial, instance, category, and subtask composition generalization across diffusion policies and VLAs. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05567) · [project](https://pairlab.github.io/MESA).
- **[RAVEL](RAVEL.md), October 31, 2026** — theoretical relative bounds and policy tournaments showing that a learned world model can preserve pairwise policy rankings while still failing at closed-loop policy optimization or paired audit variance reduction. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01139).
- **[Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI](Physical-AI-RSI.md), October 28, 2026** — a survey and evaluation framework separating learner state updates from improver revisions with an L0–L3 evidence rubric (task-local adaptation, persistent capability gain, inherited process revision, and verified improver gain). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38266) · [catalog](https://github.com/nkd-lkz/physical-ai-rsi-survey).
- **[OopsieVerse](OopsieVerse.md), June 30, 2026 (RSS 2026)** — introduces `DamageSim` across OmniGibson and RoboCasa to quantify mechanical, thermal, and fluid damage during robot manipulation and train damage-conditioned RL/IL policies. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31993) · [project](https://robin-lab.cs.utexas.edu/oopsieverse/).
- **[WorldArena](WorldArena.md), February 9, 2026** — a benchmark separating visual quality from functional world-model usefulness. The original study uses simulated manipulation; it is not an RL algorithm or proof of general hardware transfer. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08971).
- **[Xiaomi-Robotics-1](Xiaomi-Robotics-1.md), July 16, 2026** — large-scale VLA data and adaptation. Useful foundation-policy context, but the checked description does not establish RL as the source of its gains. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330).
- **[Gemini Robotics 2](Gemini-Robotics-2.md), July 30, 2026** — official whole-body/dexterity/multi-robot announcement. Its inspected release does not specify an RL method; keep these model capabilities distinct from RL algorithm evidence. [Official release](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/).
- **Physical Intelligence π0.7, April 16, 2026** — the official release describes distilling experience from earlier RECAP optimization into a general policy using strategy metadata. This is useful context for retaining and sharing learned behaviors, not a claim that all training is online RL. [Official release](https://www.pi.website/blog/pi07).

### Beyond robotics: 2026 agent, reasoning, and core conference RL

- **[Accelerating Safe RL with Massive Parallelism](Parallel-Safe-RL.md), November 5, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026)** — reconstructs unbiased full-episode cost returns from staggered short-horizon parallel rollouts with uncertainty-aware CMDP constraint tightening in MJX Safety Gym. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08273).
- **[Permanent and Transient Representations for Continual RL](PTR-CRL.md), November 5, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026)** — pairs slow parametric permanent value representations with fast non-parametric transient value estimators on Craftax-Classic and image-based continual RL. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07327).
- **[Reward Budgeting](Reward-Budgeting.md), November 4, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026)** — assigns a fixed per-prompt reward budget across rollouts to yield diminishing marginal advantage gaps as accuracy rises, curbing premature distribution sharpening on AIME24/25 and AMC23. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05942).
- **[Rubric Curriculum RL (RcRL)](RcRL.md), November 4, 2026 (ICML 2026)** — combines pairwise rollout preferences with a non-stationary rubric curriculum to prevent judge reward hacking in open-ended domains (HealthBench and creative writing). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06244).
- **[ARPG](ARPG.md), November 3, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026)** — uses LLMs as dependency-aware MDP designers and modular simulator builders before RL training. Evaluated in Isaac Sim and simulated control domains rather than physical deployment. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04974).
- **[RLiD](RLiD.md), November 2, 2026** — optimizes probabilities over compiled action menus in a single forward pass and distills trajectories into generative LLMs, avoiding token-level realization variance on discrete-menu agent tasks such as ALFWorld. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02455).
- **[ANTE](ANTE.md), October 11, 2026** — trains coding agents to predict execution outcomes before acting and converts execution-verified beliefs into per-step credit via Belief-Anchored Policy Optimization (BAPO) on SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench 2.0. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15368).
- **[MEND](MEND.md), October 5, 2026** — reward post-training for flow-based image generators via proximal velocity matching using differentiable reward gradients without a frozen reference model. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05954) · [code](https://github.com/shreshthsaini/MEND-RL).
- **[NSPG](NSPG.md), September 7, 2026 (ICML 2026)** — introduces a noisy-space action-value function and KL-regularized noisy-space policy gradient to train diffusion policies in offline RL (D4RL and vision-based OGBench) without backpropagating through the denoising chain. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06882) · [project](https://mahmoud-selim.github.io/NSPG/).
- **[EnvACE](EnvACE.md), August 6, 2026** — jointly trains tool use and environment-response rehearsal with role-wise GRPO. Its tool-use benchmarks test digital agents, not robot dynamics. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06197) · [repository](https://github.com/Within-yao/EnvACE).
- **[Relative Value Learning](Relative-Value-Learning.md), July 23, 2026 (ICLR 2026)** — learns antisymmetric pairwise state-value differences $\Delta(s_i, s_j) = V(s_i) - V(s_j)$ via a $\gamma$-contractive pairwise Bellman operator and Relative GAE (`R-GAE`) across 49 Atari games. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21120).
- **[SHAPO](SHAPO.md), June 8, 2026 (ICLR 2026)** — uses actor parameter-perturbation sensitivity as an ensemble-free epistemic uncertainty proxy for pessimistic safe exploration. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10228).
- **[MemRL](MemRL.md), January 6, 2026 (NeurIPS 2026)** — performs non-parametric runtime RL over episodic agent memory via two-phase semantic + learned $Q$-value retrieval on ALFWorld, BigCodeBench, HLE, and Lifelong Agent Bench. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03192) · [code](https://github.com/MemTensor/MemRL).

### What to measure before adopting a result

1. Separate physical interaction time from wall-clock training, reset time, and human intervention effort.
2. Report demonstration requirements, reward construction, robot hardware, and simulator or reconstruction cost.
3. Distinguish simulated success from physical evaluation, and held-out poses from held-out tasks or environments.
4. Report trials and seeds, uncertainty, failures, collisions, and recovery behavior. Do not compare headline success rates from different task suites.
5. For learned worlds, test action consistency and downstream control, not only convincing video. For real robots, test retention after updates and deployment failure modes.
6. Check whether training code, checkpoints, data, and hardware definitions are actually available. This guide records observed availability without asserting reproducibility.

## Earlier foundations to retain (2023–2025)

These remain useful context, but their original releases are not new 2026 results:

- **DeepSeek-R1** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948). A major RL-driven reasoning milestone for LLMs, using RL for reasoning, followed by additional training and distillation stages.
- **Open X-Embodiment** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864) · [dataset](https://robotics-transformer-x.github.io/). A key large-scale dataset and benchmark for cross-robot policy learning.
- **RT-2 / vision-language-action models** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15818). A strong example of decision-making policies grounded in pretrained vision-language models.
- **V-JEPA** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471). Self-supervised video feature prediction (2024), an adjacent representation-learning method rather than an RL algorithm.
- **Eureka** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12931). Reward design from language models, showing how LLMs can help create RL objectives and feedback signals.
- **OpenVLA** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246). One of the clearest open generalist robot policy stacks in the RL/robotics ecosystem.

## Canonical RL foundations

These papers remain the reference stack even as the field expands into LLMs and embodied systems:

- **Q-learning** — Watkins & Dayan (1992). The foundation for temporal-difference control. [paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00992698)
- **Temporal Difference Learning** — Sutton (1988). The conceptual engine of modern RL. [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/327200)
- **DQN** — Mnih et al. (2015). The deep RL breakthrough that revived large-scale value-based learning. [paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14236)
- **PPO** — Schulman et al. (2017). The most widely used stable on-policy optimization baseline. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347)
- **SAC** — Haarnoja et al. (2018). A standard continuous-control baseline with entropy-regularized policy learning. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290)
- **AlphaGo / AlphaZero** — Silver et al. (2016, 2017). Landmark self-play and planning work in high-dimensional sequential decision tasks. [paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16961)
- **MuZero** — Schrittwieser et al. (2020). A foundational model-based RL system for planning with learned dynamics. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08265)
- **World Models** — Ha & Schmidhuber (2018). A classic bridge between latent environment models and control. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122)
- **Inverse Reinforcement Learning** — Ng & Russell (2000). Still foundational for reward learning and preference-based control. [paper](https://ai.stanford.edu/~ang/papers/icml00-irl.pdf)

## LLM and agentic RL

The strongest modern RL work is increasingly coupled to language, planning, and tool use:

- **RLHF** — Ouyang et al. (2022). A major alignment pattern in which preference learning is used to optimize language models. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)
- **DPO** — Rafailov et al. (2023). Preference optimization without a separate reward model. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290)
- **GRPO / reasoning RL** — DeepSeek-R1-style reasoning pipelines and GRPO-type optimization show the newest RL-for-reasoning direction. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948)
- **ReAct** — Yao et al. (2023). A canonical reasoning-and-action pattern for language-agent task execution. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) · [code](https://github.com/ysymyth/ReAct)
- **WebArena** — Zhou et al. (2023). A realistic benchmark for long-horizon browser agents and tool use. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854) · [code](https://github.com/web-arena-x/webarena)
- **OpenVLA / Octo / RT-2 class systems** — foundation policies for embodied control and instruction-conditioned decision making. [OpenVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246) · [Octo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12213)

This is the frontier where “LLM agent” and “RL” meet: the system is tasked with acting over multiple steps, integrating tool feedback, and optimizing a decision policy rather than just generating a single completion.

## A practical reading path

1. Start with **D4RL** and **Procgen** to understand data quality and generalization.
2. Read one classical reference: **DQN**, **PPO**, or **SAC**.
3. Learn one model-based baseline: **DreamerV3** or **TD-MPC2**.
4. Learn the VLA starting point with **OpenVLA** or **Octo**, then compare compact representation routing (**RL Token**, **eRLT**), sim-to-real residual control (**Object-Centric Residual RL**, **VLaRL**), and persistent or long-horizon refinement (**FIND**, **AtomicRL**, **DRIVE**, **VLA-Stem**, **PolicyIK**).
5. Read **WorldArena**, **RAVEL**, **CRONOS**, and **Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI** for evaluation boundaries, and compare **HaWMPO**, **WoVR+**, and **WorldSample** on handling unreliable imagined experience. Use **FMAPPO** to explore embodied multi-agent coordination.
6. For language-agent and generative RL, compare **ARPG**, **RLiD**, **ANTE**, and **EnvACE** (plus **MEND** for flow-model post-training) with older **DeepSeek-R1**, **RLHF**, and **DPO**. DPO is preference optimization; it does not use the same online interaction loop.
7. Before claiming a result, report evaluation, robustness, and safety as carefully as the algorithm itself.
