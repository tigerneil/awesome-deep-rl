# Deep RL frontier guide — 2026

This is a deliberately small, high-signal companion to the historical catalog. It is organized around capabilities that are shaping current RL systems: learning from data, imagining with world models, acting through tools, coordinating with other agents, and deploying safely in the physical world. Links point to primary papers or the authors' official code where available.

> **How to use this guide:** pick one theme, read the anchor paper, reproduce a baseline, then follow the benchmark and code links. The [interactive homepage](index.html) remembers what you have explored locally.

## Foundation and world models

- **DreamerV3** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104) · [code](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv3). A strong, general world-model baseline with fixed hyperparameters across domains.
- **TD-MPC2** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16828) · [code](https://github.com/nicklashansen/tdmpc2). Latent planning and scalable offline-to-online control for continuous tasks.
- **Genie: Generative Interactive Environments** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15391). A useful bridge between generative video models and interactive environment models.

## Offline, hybrid, and data-centric RL

- **D4RL** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07219) · [benchmark](https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/D4RL). The canonical starting point for offline datasets and evaluation.
- **RLPD: Reinforcement Learning with Prior Data** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02948) · [code](https://github.com/ikostrikov/rlpd). A practical recipe for mixing logged data with online interaction.
- **Decision Transformer** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01345) · [code](https://github.com/kzl/decision-transformer). The sequence-modeling view of offline control remains an essential conceptual reference.

## Robotics and foundation policies

- **OpenVLA** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246) · [code](https://github.com/openvla/openvla). An open vision-language-action policy and fine-tuning stack.
- **Octo** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12213) · [code](https://github.com/octo-models/octo). An open generalist robot policy designed for multi-task adaptation.
- **Xiaomi Robotics-1** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330). A 2026 example of the field's shift toward large real-world robot data pipelines and reusable VLA systems.

## Agents, language, and tools

- **ReAct** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) · [code](https://github.com/ysymyth/ReAct). The canonical interleaving of reasoning, action, and observation.
- **WebArena** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854) · [benchmark](https://github.com/web-arena-x/webarena). A realistic benchmark for long-horizon browser/tool-use agents.
- **AgentBench** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688) · [code](https://github.com/THUDM/AgentBench). Multi-environment evaluation for LLM agents with decision-making and tool use.

## Multi-agent coordination

- **JaxMARL** — [code](https://github.com/FLAIROx/JaxMARL). Fast, reproducible multi-agent environments and baselines.
- **Learning Bilateral Team Formation in Cooperative Multi-Agent RL** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20039). Dynamic team formation under changing roles and objectives.
- **MAPPO** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01955) · [code](https://github.com/marlbenchmark/on-policy). A strong, widely reproduced on-policy baseline for cooperative MARL.

## Generalization, safety, and evaluation

- **Procgen** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01588) · [code](https://github.com/openai/procgen). Procedural generalization tests that expose memorization.
- **Safety Gym** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.01745) · [code](https://github.com/openai/safety-gym). A canonical constrained-RL benchmark for safe exploration.
- **Empirical Design in Reinforcement Learning** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01396). A reminder to report seeds, compute, evaluation protocols, and uncertainty rather than single impressive runs.

## A practical 2026 reading path

1. Start with **D4RL** and **Procgen** to understand data quality and generalization.
2. Learn one model-based baseline with **DreamerV3** or **TD-MPC2**.
3. Move to embodied policies with **OpenVLA** or **Octo**.
4. Evaluate tool use with **ReAct** and **WebArena**.
5. Reproduce a cooperative baseline with **MAPPO**, then explore dynamic team formation.
6. Before claiming a result, report the evaluation and safety practices from the final section.
